# 3D worlds

Two worlds built from `src/three/dims.ts` (metres, the only source of dimensions: every constant says `reported`, `derived`, `sim` or
`estimate`; whatever `src/sim` derives is imported, never retyped) with the frozen ids of `src/part-ids.ts`: `site` 12 parts, `screw` 20 parts.
`src/three/index.ts` exports `createSiteScene()` and `createScrewScene(opts?)`, both `lazyModule`s (nothing is built until the world is shown).
Checked by `*.test.ts` beside the code (see Tests). The screw is **generic**: sized by the published design rules at the lab's design point
(1.75 m, 180 L/s: outer diameter 0.86 m, inner 0.46 m, pitch 0.86 m, length 4.67 m, slope 22°, 3 threads, gap 4.2 mm), never drawn as the
association's machine; no brand, person or company name appears in any label.

## Frames

- **screw world, world frame**: X horizontal along the screw (the upper end and the drive on the +X side, the water flows towards -X), Y up, Z
  towards the viewer; origin on the floor under the lower end of the flights. The **screw frame** is the world frame tilted by the slope about Z
  and moved to A0 (the axis at the lower end of the flights): X' along the axis towards the upper end, Y' up, Z across. The section plane is z = 0.
- **thread**: RIGHT-HANDED (x' = x0 + pitch ψ / 2π, ψ from +Y' towards +Z). The turbine turns in the positive sense about +X' (`rotation.x = +angle`),
  which makes the pockets of water go down the slope. Both facts are tested on the geometry (`helix.test.ts`, `screw.test.ts`).
- **site frame**: X east, Y up, Z south (towards the visitor), datum Y = 0 the design level of the headwater; the river and the canal flow towards -X.
  The tailrace is **compressed**: about 21 m and the turn to the river, where the real one is about 100 m (S1 12:36); everything else is at true scale.

Scenery (not registered, the supports of the "no floating part" test): the platform and its posts, the plywood board on its legs, the cables, the
paper floor; plus `trapped-water`'s **fish** (a small fish that rides a pocket, visible in the section; `ownerPart = 'scenery'`, never picked).

(the shafts are parallel to the axis, so they climb 0.37 m per metre above the flat top): the generator's plinth is about 0.5 m high.

### What `update(state, dt, view)` reads

| State                                                           | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `angle` (rad, already slowed)                                   | unwrapped each frame; turns core, blades, fasteners, then every wheel by `angle × its ratio`; moves the pockets (one blade spacing per third of a turn), the caps of the blades in the section, the fish; scrolls the chain and belt patterns                                  |
| `omega`, `view.slowMo`                                          | visible rate of every wheel (the wagon-wheel guard, below) and the speed of the water dots (`pitch × rev/s`)                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `fill` (0..1)                                                   | the bucket is rebuilt when it moves by 0.012 (the level of a bucket is `hMin + fill (hPass - hMin)`), the polygon of the section too, the level of the basin (`fill` = 1 is the lowest saddle, less water lowers the pool, as the simulation's drawdown); no water under 0.015 |
| `tailRise`                                                      | top of the outlet pool = design tail level (`0.6 D cos β` over the lowest point of the trough) + `tailRise`; the pool is murkier when flooded                                                                                                                                  |
| `outerDiameter`, `innerDiameter`, `pitch`, `length`, `slopeDeg` | the world is **rebuilt** at that size when one changes by 0.4 % (at most every 0.15 s: about 15 to 25 ms of work, geometry only, the registered groups stay, `PartEntry.meshes` is refreshed)                                                                                  |
| `rpm`, `generatorRpm`                                           | without the sim's ratios (see below) the belt ratio is `generatorRpm / rpm` over the default chain ratio                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `charging`, `electricPower`                                     | lamp of the controller, chase of the five segments of the battery                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `current` (A)                                                   | speed of the dots of the current (0.03 m/s per ampere along the cables, 0.022 on the line out)                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `view.cutaway`, `view.schematic`                                | the section (below), the "Flux" dots: water first (down the pockets, the basin and the pool), then the current from the generator to the battery and out                                                                                                                       |
| zeros in the size (`machine` is a wheel)                        | **the screw is left drawn at rest**: no rotation, no water, no rebuild; nothing is drawn for the wheel (a comparison only, no part, no world)                                                                                                                                  |

**Wagon-wheel guard.** A wheel has `symmetry` identical positions per turn (teeth, holes, 1 for a plain disc). Above 14 positions a second a
translucent disc fades in (full at 24) and the wheel stops following the true angle: it moves at most 14 positions a second. The big sprocket, the
small sprocket, the two pulleys have a disc; the shafts and the generator stub are plain. The patterns of the chain and the belt never run faster than 12 repeats
a second. At the design point (55 rpm) the small sprocket (17 teeth, 3 rev/s) is blurred, the generator pulley (10 rev/s) too, the big sprocket and
the screw are not.

**Ratios.** `createScrewScene({ ratios: () => ({ chain, belt }) })` takes the live ratios (the lead passes `sim.params.chainRatio` and `.beltRatio`); without it the
chain ratio is the default of the simulation for that diameter (`defaultDriveRatios`, the arithmetic of `plantFor`) and the belt takes the rest of `generatorRpm / rpm`. A
change redraws the small sprocket (teeth = 56 / ratio), the small pulley (radius = large / ratio), the chain and the belt.

### The section (`view.cutaway`)

The plane is the vertical plane through the axis (z = 0); the half towards the viewer is removed from the materials of `trough`, `screw-core`,
`blade-module`, `blade-fastener`, `inlet-sill`, `outlet-pool` and `trapped-water` (and nothing of the drive, which stands beside the axis). Caps close
what is cut: the trough's bottom strip and the piers, the core tube and the shaft, **the blades as radial rectangles that follow the angle** (the helicoid is
ruled: its section by a plane through the axis is a straight radial line, so the staircase of blades above and below the axis is exact), the headwall, the slab and
the wall of the pool, and the water: the pocket's polygon (a rectangle between two blades while the bucket is full, a wedge under the level line
when it is not) and the rectangles of the two pools. A test requires that every pocket is bounded, at five angles, by the face of one blade on one side and the
face of the next on the other.

### Explode, x-ray, labels, camera

Explode (`EXPLODE` in `screw/scene.ts`, in the frame of the screw, scaled by the hardware scale): `trough` 1.6 m towards the viewer, then the three layers
`screw-core` 1.1, `blade-module` 2.0, `blade-fastener` 2.9 m along the normal, `trapped-water` 3.2 m towards the viewer, the pools along the axis or
back, the bearings and the sprocket along the shaft, the chain, belt and countershaft up, the electronics off the board. X-ray with a part selected ghosts the rest (the
water and its blades read well); the lead gives `xrayShell` none for this world. Main labels (four): wooden blades, trapped water, chain, trough
(`blade-module`, `trapped-water`, `chain`, `trough`); the other sixteen are minor. They are placed so that none touches another at 842×678, 842×736,
380×902 and 390×632 in both languages (`labels.test.ts`, with a replica of the kit's framing). An invisible point 1.3 m over the generator gives the overview
the headroom for the top label (the fit counts the parts, not the labels).

## For the lead: `src/lab.ts`

- Scenes: `{ id: 'site', module: createSiteScene(), cutaway: false, xrayNeedsSelection: true }` and `{ id: 'screw', module: createScrewScene({ ratios: () => ({ chain: sim.params.chainRatio, belt: sim.params.beltRatio }) }) }`
  (both are `lazyModule`s already: do not wrap them again). The kit treats the id `site` as a landscape (authored pose, far plane 4000).
- `partLinks`: `'water-chamber'` → `{ scene: 'screw', label: … }` (the hotspot already carries `goto: 'screw'`).
- `xrayShell`: site `['mill-building', 'house-load']` (in `WORLD.site`: the roofs hide the yard from above); screw none.
- `WORLD.site` and `WORLD.screw` (exported by `src/three/index.ts`) hold the `SceneDef` fields to spread: `cutaway`, `xrayNeedsSelection`, `partPadding` (site 3, screw 2.4),
  `stepFrame` (site `{ padding: 1.6, minRadius: 1.5 }`, screw `{ padding: 1.6, minRadius: 0.3 }`) and the site's `xrayShell`. `homePadding` for the screw is 0.8, set in the module.
- `cutaway`: `true` for `screw`, `false` for `site`. No `onFrame`, no `onCutaway` hook: both worlds apply `view.cutaway` and `view.schematic` in `update` (the kit's warm-up
  switches the section on through `update`, which is enough).
- The simulation's clock used to start at 00:00 (the first report of this role); the lead fixed it: the clock now starts on 1 October at 12:00 (at 08:00 the sun is on the horizon at 45.5° N, so the landscape would open at dawn). The 3D reads `hour` as it is.

## Tests (`npx vitest run src/three`)

`dims.test.ts` (8): finite numbers, the screw is the one the simulation sizes (nothing retyped), the drive arithmetic (ratios, teeth, radii, centre distances), the scale of the
hardware, the wheel fallback, the compressed tailrace. `screw/helix.test.ts` (10): faces look outwards, right-handed thread, module count, the bucket against the simulation's table (levels, depth, volume
within 10 %), pocket buffers never reallocated, free surface horizontal, pockets go down with the right sense, section polygons. `screw/screw.test.ts` (28): exact ids and meshes,
labels, no floating part and what stands on what, the true size and the half-immersed level, the chain and belt tangent to both wheels and in their plane, the shafts that carry the wheels and
are parallel to the axis, the ratios, the rotation of every wheel, the wagon-wheel guard, the pattern's direction, the pockets bounded by two blades at five angles, the pools, the fish, the dots, the
section (caps, clipped parts, blade caps that follow the angle), explode, isolate and ghost for every part, a NaN sweep over head, flow, slope, flood, low water, closed gate, no start, ratios and a
wheel scenario, the redraw at another size and at another ratio. `site/site.test.ts` (20): exact ids and meshes, labels, no floating part (against the land tiles), what stands where, the pond and
the weir formula, the tailwater, the fish pass steps, the rack against the simulation's loss, the gate, the drawdown, the streaks' speed, the chamber's screw (angle, fill, pools, redraw, wheel), the lamps,
the sun, the dots, explode, a NaN sweep, the budget. `labels.test.ts` (16): the main labels of both worlds apart and inside the view at four sizes in both languages. `merge.test.ts` (4): merged and
unmerged builds have the same bounds and triangles per part. Total 86 tests.

Budget (colour pass, counted from the scene graph; `renderer.info` is for the lead to confirm): **site 76 draw calls (+ 49 shadow casters), 33,000 triangles** (limit 300 and 250,000); **screw 83 draw calls
(+ 72), 47,000 triangles** (94 draw calls with the section and the dots). Building the screw world takes about 20 ms, redrawing it at another size about 15 ms.
`merge.ts` bakes the static meshes of a part into one mesh per material (colours in the vertices, except for the parts the section clips, which keep their own materials), the turning groups inside their own
frame; no geometry or material is created per frame (the pocket, the pools, the section caps and the dots write into buffers allocated once).

## What is estimated, what is simplified

- **Estimates** (all tagged in `dims.ts`): every size that is not a result of the simulation: the trough (7 cm wall, 0.3 R sides), the piers, the bearings, the platform and the pools, the chain (ISO 16B, 25.4 mm, 56 teeth),
  the pulleys (0.40 m), the countershaft (44 mm), the generator (R 0.155 m), the electronics, the weir, the notch, the fish pass, the canal, the rack (8 cm spacing), the gate, the chamber, the buildings. The hardware of a
  screw of another size is scaled by `(D / 0.86)^0.7`, between 0.6 and 2.2 (purely visual). **Handedness** (right) and the number of modules (a quarter turn each) are choices: the sources show neither.
- **Simplified**: the helicoid blade is a thick ruled slab (5 steps per quarter turn, the tip a polygon); the water pockets ignore the 4 mm gap and keep the blade thickness out of the volume, so the section's full bucket is a little
  less than the simulation's volume (checked within 10 %); the pocket's outer wall is the blade tip; the pool and basin are boxes (the basin's polygon is exact in the section); the screw in the site is the same geometry at 0.7 detail without drive or bearings
  (a box stands for the drive); the weir is a wall with a sheet of water, not a nappe; the chain is a band with a dashed pattern (no links, no pins), the belt a flat band (no V); sprocket teeth are trapezoids; the rack and the gate have no hinges; the
  river has no waves, only streaks; trees are icospheres. The platform is flat (see above); the generator's rotor is not drawn; the electronics are boxes with terminals and cables, not circuits.
- **Not drawn**: guards over the chain and the belt (a real machine needs them, the lab's text says so), the ladder and the hoist of the installation, ice, sediment, the fish ladder's baffles, the real stage-discharge of the river.

# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` lacks, from the physics and panel work of Mill Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. Nothing here was worked around by patching the kit; each item says what the lab did instead.

1. **Tests of a panel need Node to import the kit.** The kit's `dist` uses extensionless relative imports (`./lang`, `./electric`), so a vitest file that imports
   `@onigam/lab-kit/i18n` or `/sim` fails in Node unless the lab adds `test.server.deps.inline: ['@onigam/lab-kit']` to its Vite config (Piggott Lab does; Mill Lab had to,
   in `vite.config.ts`, which is outside the physics role's files). Please publish ESM with extensions (or a build that Node resolves) so that no lab needs the line, and the
   barrel `@onigam/lab-kit/sim` can be imported (the lab imports `@onigam/lab-kit/sim/hydraulic` for now).
2. **A fake DOM for panel tests.** `src/panels/test-dom.ts` (a small stand-in for the DOM that the kit's `h`, `fill`, widgets and charts call) is copied from Piggott Lab; ram-pump-lab
   has its own. Ship one in the kit (for example `@onigam/lab-kit/testing`) with the same `installFakeDom()` so that panels are tested identically in every lab.
3. **`num(value, digits)` and `months()`.** Every lab writes the same locale-aware number formatter (`7,5` / `7.5`, no `-0,0`); Mill Lab also needs short month names. Put them in
   `@onigam/lab-kit/i18n`, with a `LAB_STRINGS` export convention for the parity test (every lab's `strings.test.ts` is the same file with a different list of allowed numbers).
4. **Charts: log axes, filled bands and polygons, custom ticks.** The chooser chart (head against flow, log-log, zones that overlap, lines of equal power) had to be faked with
   `LineChart`: log10 values on linear axes, every zone as a closed outline drawn by giving each series its own stretch of a shared list of abscissas (`NaN` elsewhere), `legend: false` and a
   hand-made legend. The four tick labels at thirds of the axis forced the lab to choose maxima divisible by three (`niceCeil3`). Please add: a log scale option, `fill` for a series
   (zones, bands), optional explicit ticks, and a tooltip that skips series with `NaN`.
5. **`section(title, …)` that can start closed.** The kit's `section` always opens (`open: true`); the panels that fold their secondary controls set `.open = false` on the returned
   element. An `open` argument would say it.
6. **A `readout` widget.** The `.readouts` grid of boxes (label, value, unit) is copied by every panel, and its label is cut with an ellipsis at about 14 characters, so the lab shows
   anything longer as a line in the `.loss-legend` grid instead. A kit widget could allow two-line labels and take a list of `{label, value, unit}`.
7. **`sim/mech` to move into the kit.** `src/sim/mech.ts` (chain and belt stages, torque `P/ω`, a permanent-magnet generator on a battery with voltage ∝ speed and torque ∝ current,
   `matchSpeed`) is pure, in SI, free of any lab import and tested (`mech.test.ts`): the gravity battery and Piggott's alternator can reuse it. Piggott's alternator and this one share the
   bridge factor 1.35 and the diode drop; a common `generatorAt` would remove the copy.
8. **Time controls beyond an hour.** The strip's default speeds stop at ×3600; a lab that follows the seasons wants a day or a week per second. The lab passes `speeds: [1, 3600, 86400, 604800]`
   to the strip; the label is fine, but a long clock (a date, not 00:00) deserves a kit helper for `clock()`.
9. **A shared yearly chart.** Piggott's Weibull year, the mill's flow year and the future labs' seasonal charts are all "365 values with a marker for today": a small kit component would also
   give the day-of-year axis labels in both languages.
10. **The 3D helpers that every lab copies.** Mill Lab's `src/three` copies from Piggott Lab (itself from the ram lab) and adapts: `merge.ts` (`mergeStatic`, `dynamic`, the colour baking), `cut.ts` (the section tool: a clipping plane,
    flat caps drawn from the 2D section of each part, `registerCap`, `reset`), `framing.ts` (a replica of `Stage.frameAll` and of the label CSS, used by `labels.test.ts`), `world.ts` (`workshopScene`, `hotspots(defs, anchors)`, `anchor`),
    `common.ts` (`paperFloor`, `FlowDots`/`DotPath`, `tubeGeometry`, `waterMaterial`, `noShadow`, `clearGroup`) and `site/sky.ts` (the Preetham sky with night and a sun by the clock). Move them to the kit (`@onigam/lab-kit/three`, with the
    test helpers in `@onigam/lab-kit/testing`) so that a fix is made once; `framing.ts` in particular must follow the kit's `Stage`, and a copy per lab will drift.
11. **A world that is rebuilt at run time.** Mill Lab redraws its screw when the visitor changes the design point (a different diameter, length, slope). `PartRegistry.add` collects the meshes of a part once (and refuses a second `add` of the id), so the lab keeps
    each registered group, clears and refills it, and refreshes `entry.meshes`, `userData.ownerPart` and `userData.solidMaterial` by hand (and re-applies the display mode). A `registry.refresh(id)` that does this (keeping the explode factor and the display) would remove a fragile copy of the registry's internals.
12. **Room for labels in the overview.** The overview fits the parts, not their labels, so the label of the highest part needs an invisible `Points` object added to a part ("headroom", `docs/3d.md`) to stay in the picture when the narration bar or a sheet covers the view, and a label that is read to the
    right of its dot (the longest, "Générateur", on a phone) cannot be given room on the right at all. A `SceneModule.labelRoom` (pixels or metres on each side) taken into account by `frameAll`, or labels that flip to the left of their dot near the right edge, would remove both workarounds.
13. **The first frame of the stage in headless Chrome.** With puppeteer and SwiftShader the first `render(dt)` receives a negative `dt` (about -2 s), the camera flight started by the first `frameAll(0.01)` gets `t = -199` and the camera is thrown to 10⁷ m until a second framing: the overview then
    shows nothing for about 4 s. `Stage.render` should clamp `dt` to `[0, 0.05]` (a real browser would show it too after a long pause of the tab). The lab's own screenshots call `stage.frameAll(0.01)` once more after the first frames.
14. **The drawing helpers every lab copies.** Mill Lab's `src/drawings` copies from Piggott Lab (itself from the ram lab) and adapts: `draw.ts` (`wrap`, `note`, `heading`, `caption`, `grid` (a table with wrapped cells), `box`, `marksLegend` (the legend of the dagger and asterisk marks), `balloon`, `poly`, `arrow`, `dimV`, `dimH`, `gapDim`), `chart.ts` (an SVG `Chart` with axes, ticks, polylines and markers) and
    `test-dom.ts` (the fake DOM of `Drawing` with `textBoxes` / `textExtent`, so that a test can look for text on text and outside the frame). Move them to the kit (`@onigam/lab-kit/drawings`, the fake DOM in `@onigam/lab-kit/testing`, which request 2 already asks for) so that a fix is made once; the notice that every sheet carries ("not a construction plan") is the only lab-specific part.
15. **Arrowheads and arcs on a `View`.** A curved arrow (the sense of rotation) or a line with an arrowhead has to be built with `view.polyline(...)` and `el.setAttribute('marker-end', 'url(#arrow)')`, which depends on the id of the kit's own marker. `View.arrow(a, b, style)` and an `arrow: 'start' | 'end' | 'both'` option of `arc` and `polyline` would say it; `View.dim` would also gain an option to put the label at a chosen fraction of the line (a label that is always centred falls on a pier or a balloon).
16. **A `concrete` hatch and bilingual scale cells.** The kit's hatches have no concrete: the labs use `earth` (dots) for concrete and for the ground alike, so the two cannot be told apart in a section. A `concrete` (dots and small triangles) and a `sand` would do. `TitleBlock.scale` is a plain string while every other cell is bilingual: a sheet with two scales prints `1:35 · 1:10` and has no room for a third (the cell fits 12 characters).
17. **The water of the 3D worlds, as a kit module.** Mill Lab's `src/three/water` (`docs/water.md`) is generic: a flow-advected water material (a `MeshStandardMaterial` patched through `onBeforeCompile`, so the kit's display modes and the section keep working) driven by one procedural 256² noise texture, a grid body with a painted foam channel, a nappe (a free-falling sheet from the head and the unit discharge) and a ring-buffer spray, with no asset and no extra pass. Pelton's jets and tailwater, Piggott's ram and the next hydraulic labs redraw the same water by hand (or with a flat translucent plane): a shared `@onigam/lab-kit/three/water` would give every lab the same look and one place to fix. The lab's own `waterMaterial` in `common.ts` (request 10) would then go.
18. **Drawables that are not meshes in the registry.** A world's spray, drops and flow dots are `THREE.Points`: `PartRegistry.setDisplay` swaps the material of `PartEntry.meshes` only, so a part that is hidden, ghosted or exploded leaves its spray hanging in the air, and every lab hides it by hand (Mill Lab reads `registry.get(id).display` and `.factor` every frame and zeroes the alpha of the drops). `PartEntry` could list `points: THREE.Points[]` that follow the same display and explode rules (hidden when the part is not solid, moved with the part).
19. **A section that is open at first.** The generator world's hero view is the section (a quarter of the machine taken away), but the closed machine of the overview hides the parts, and `view.cutaway` starts off in every world. A `SceneDef.cutawayDefault` (or a way for a world to ask the stage for its section when it is shown) would open the world on its best picture.
20. **Picking must understand `clipIntersection`.** `Stage.cast` skips a hit when `mat.clippingPlanes.some(plane clips the point)`. A material with `clipIntersection: true` (a quarter cut away: the front half AND beyond a plane across) is clipped only where ALL its planes clip, so with the kit's test the front-left half of the machine cannot be picked. Mill Lab's `QuarterCut` gives every material an array whose `some` is `every` to get the right answer; the kit should test `clipIntersection` itself. The outline pass has the same blind spot: it draws the outline of a clipped-away part.
21. **A quarter-section tool in the kit.** Mill Lab's `src/three/generator/cut.ts` (two clipping planes with `clipIntersection`, moved with an exploded part, flat caps drawn from the 2D section of each part, caps that a part shows and hides itself) is the section of a machine with an axis: the half cut along the axis hides the rotor's cross-section, the quarter shows both. Put `QuarterCut` next to `CutKit` (request 10) and let `SceneDef.cutaway` choose `'half' | 'quarter'`.
22. **Walls that fade with the camera.** The powerroom world is a room seen from outside through its front wall and from inside through its walls: each enclosing surface fades when the camera is on its outer side (`onBeforeRender` setting the opacity of a transparent material, never casting a shadow). Piggott Lab's shed does the same by hand; a kit helper `fadeWhenOutside(mesh, test)` and a `SceneModule.interior` hint for the overview framing (a world whose home pose is inside its volume) would say it once.
23. **Logarithmic axes, filled zones and polygons in the SVG `Chart` of the sheets.** The turbine chart of PL-08 (head against flow, both logarithmic, eight overlapping zones with a light fill and a strong outline, lines of equal power) cannot use the sheets' `Chart` helper (linear axes, polylines only): the sheet maps the logarithms by hand and draws each zone twice (a fill at 20 % opacity, then its outline), because `Stroke.opacity` fades the outline with the fill. A `Chart` with `scale: 'log'` per axis, a `zone(points, { fill, stroke })` and graduations at the decades and their multiples would remove it; `Stroke` could take `fillOpacity` apart from `opacity`.
24. **A polygon with holes on a `View`.** The lamination of a stator (a disc with a star of 24 slots cut out of it, PL-04) is drawn as the disc, then the hole polygon filled with the paper colour on top, which paints over anything under it and cannot be hatched round the hole. `View.polygon(outer, { holes: [...] })` (fill rule even-odd) would draw the section as it is, and `View.clip(...)` a detail of a wedge (the tooth and the pole of PL-04 E is built by hand from the boundary of the wedge).
25. **A set of electrical symbols for single-line diagrams.** Piggott's PL-07 and Mill's `src/drawings/symbols.ts` each draw the same few symbols in paper space (generator, three-wire slashes, breaker, fuse, rectifier bridge, battery, resistor, meter, house, earth, surge arrester, controlled switch): `@onigam/lab-kit/drawings/symbols` with a common line weight and colour convention (three-phase, direct, alternating, earth) would keep every lab's diagram the same; the colours of the phases (red, yellow, blue as the 3D sleeves them) are in `draw.ts` here and in Piggott's `PHASE`.
26. **`View.dimDiameter` that puts its label outside the circle, and `View.leader` that takes a paper target.** `dimDiameter` writes its label at the end of a short tail beyond the far side of the circle, which falls inside the drawing when the circle is the bore of a stator (PL-04 B); the sheet draws a horizontal dimension under the foot instead. A `labelAt: Pt` option, and a `leader` whose target is a paper point (the details drawn in paper space have no model point), would remove two workarounds; `sectionMark` could also take the letter's side.
27. **A bilingual `TitleBlock.scale` for a schematic.** PL-06 and PL-08 have no scale: they print an em dash in the cell (request 16 asks for bilingual scale cells; a `scale: 'none'` that the kit writes « sans échelle » / "not to scale" would say it).

# Drawings of the Mill lab (wave 3: three sheets; completion wave B: eight)

Eight A3 sheets of the "Plans" mode (the lab standard asks for eight: the owner's "Lite" of wave 3 is retired), generated from `src/three/dims.ts`, `src/sim` and `src/data/figures.ts`, never typed. Each is a kit
`DrawingSheet`, exported in reading order by `SHEETS` in `src/drawings/index.ts`. Every text is `l(fr, en)` in its own file; nothing is added to `src/i18n/ui.ts`. 125 tests in
`src/drawings/drawings.test.ts` (both languages). The first sections below are wave 3 (PL-01 to PL-03); the last one, "Completion wave B", adds PL-04 to PL-08 and fixes PL-03.

## Marks and sources of the numbers

- **Marks after a number.** none = given by a published source (3 threads, 22°, 0.5357, 0.0045, 50 rpm rule, 100 m tailrace of the video's mill); `†` = derived by the published design rules at the design point (diameters, pitch, gap, bucket,
  speed, ratios, 180 L/s); `*` = estimate of the 3D model or assumption of the model (every length of the site, the drive, the head, the length of the screw). A model result carries no mark: it sits in a red box "model result · not a measurement".
  `STATUS` and `MARKED_LOWER` in `geom.ts` hold the table; the test reads the comments of `dims.ts` (and the `REPORTED` of `machine.ts`) and fails if a sheet marks higher than the comment.
- **Lowered on purpose.** `DESIGN.head` (the comment says derived, `figures.ts` says assumed: inside the video's 1.5 to 2 m) and `SCREW.length` (head / sin 22° rests on the assumption that the two submerged ends cancel, `docs/physics.md` §1.2) are printed `*`.
  **Raised, with a reason**: `DESIGN.screwRpm` has no status word in its comment ("Nagel's rule, sim"); the published rule applied to the derived diameter is printed `†` (the test requires the comment to keep mentioning the rule).
- **Lengths**: `SITE`, `DRIVE`, `GENERATOR`, `TROUGH`, `BEARING`, `POOLS` of dims.ts (all estimates), `SCREW` and `DESIGN` (derived), through `dim()`. The tailrace is drawn at its 3D length (21.1 m\*) and the sheet says the real one is about 100 m.
- **Model results (PL-03)**: `drivePoint()` is the steady point of the preset `full-size` (1.75 m, 180 L/s, river 0.22 m³/s): screw 55 rpm · 453 N·m · 2.61 kW; countershaft (after the chain, 98 %) 182 rpm · 135 N·m · 2.56 kW; generator (after the belt, 96 %)
  600 rpm · 39 N·m · 2.46 kW; rectified open-circuit 53.7 V, 46 A into the 48 V bank, 2.20 kW, 69 W in the diodes, charging from 51 rpm; the no-load runaway (180 rpm, 176 V) is quoted from `figures.ts`. The test recomputes each of them by another route
  (`solvePlant`, `screwPoint`, `loadTorqueAtSlowShaft`, `cutInOmega`), asserts the rounded values, and compares them with `MODEL`. The curves are `MillSim.getSpeedCurve`, recomputed point by point in the test and found at their coordinates in the SVG.
- **Not printed anywhere**: the prototype's 200 W and the 2 kW target (a test fails if they appear).

## Estimated or unknown

Every dimension of the site and of the hardware is an estimate of the 3D model; the screw's dimensions are derived, none is the association's (the sheets say the screw is generic and that no maker publishes its own). The inverter, the house, the controller (and its dump load)
are drawn but **not simulated**: the diagram says so; the battery is the ideal 48 V bus of the model. The tailwater is assumed equal to the river below the weir (the 3D pins it so). The weir is not on the canal's axis: its section (PL-01 C) is along the river.

## Tests

Contract (three sheets, ids, numbers, groups, worlds, parts exist in `part-ids.ts` and belong to the world, 2 to 4 sentences, no research code, internal word or name), a render in both languages without NaN, every text inside the frame and outside the title block, no text on text (estimated boxes), decimal comma and point,
the same numbers in the same places in both languages, no French word in English and the reverse, the notice (not a construction plan, the screw is generic), **every number printed is registered by a helper or allowed with its source, and a typed number fails**, the marks against the comments, the screw's frame against the 3D's layout, PL-01's rectangles
against `SITE` (river, canal, pit, weir, notch, tailrace, three buildings), PL-02's labels against `SCREW`, `DESIGN` and `FIG`, PL-03's model results and curves against `src/sim`, the band round two wheels is tangent, the number of teeth.

## Weak or doubtful

- **Legibility at 1280 × 800** relies on the kit's zoom: the smallest text is 1.6 to 1.7 mm cap height (the tables and the cards of PL-03); the width estimates of the overlap test are not the real font (the sheets were also looked at in headless Chrome with Space Grotesk).
- **PL-01 B**: the heights are enlarged ×3, so the screw looks steeper than 22°; the pit is simplified (no headwall, no platform, the basin at the canal's level) and the river is not on the profile's axis (see C).
- **PL-02**: at the design point the pockets are full, so the free surface of a pocket is not visible in section (it stands at the axis); the staircase of levels (each pocket higher than the one below) is shown by the stepped rectangles; the 4.2 mm gap is invisible at 1:5 and said so; the cut B–B is exact at the middle of a pocket only.
- **PL-03**: chain and belt are bands (no links, no V), the generator a cylinder with a terminal box, the guards are not drawn (the sheet says they are needed); the single-line diagram has no fuse or dump-load symbol (words only), those two have no part id.
- The two views of the rotation differ by the side they are seen from (PL-02 B from the drive end: counter-clockwise; PL-03 A from the lower end: clockwise): each says so.

### Where every number comes from, and what is estimated

- **Every length** is read from `dims.ts` through `dim()` and printed with its mark: the whole `GENERATOR`, `GENERATOR_DESIGN` and `POWERROOM` blocks (and `ELECTRONICS`) are estimates of the 3D role (`*`); the screw's are those of wave 3 (`†` derived, `*` estimated).
  Not retyped: the five magic numbers of the 3D that are not in `dims.ts` (the shaft ends 30 mm behind the body, the base plate overlaps the tube by 2 mm and has half a width of 0.78 R plus half a foot, the posts of the rack stand 4 cm outside the battery, the trunking is 5 cm shorter than the board each side) are **drawing choices** of `gen.ts` and `pl05-layout.ts`; the test compares those of `gen.ts` with the 3D.
- **Estimates of the data role, marked `*` from `figures.ts`**: 16 poles, 24 slots, 8 coils per phase, 80 Hz (and `120°*` between neighbouring teeth: 15° × 8 pole pairs), the breaker 63 A, the fuses 80 A, the inverter 3 kW, the battery cables 25 mm² and the bonding 16 mm², the 48 V bank, the diodes' 1.5 V drop, the 75 % efficiency of the lines of PL-08, the flow of the mountain plant (508 L/s at the Pelton efficiency of the chooser, from its 987 kW and 220 m).
- **Derived `†`** (arithmetic on model results, tested): the voltage between two phases 39.8 V (53.7 / 1.35), a phase 23 V, 37 A a phase, the inverter's 63 A on the battery side, the dump load's 1.05 Ω, the flow 180 L/s of the design point.
- **Model results** (red boxes "MODEL RESULT · not a measurement", recomputed from `src/sim` by `drawings.test.ts`, which fails loudly if the model is recalibrated): the generator at 600 rpm and 39 N·m, 53.7 V rectified at the open terminals, 46 A and 2.20 kW into the battery, 176 V with the screw racing (PL-06 safety); for PL-07 the leak through the gap (9 L/s, 155 W) and the screw's efficiency with the gap of the rule (84.6 %), half (86.8 %), twice (80.5 %) and four times (73 %) as large.
- **Reported (no mark)**: the three threads, 22°, the heads of the zones of PL-08 (`ZONES`, published sources), the two plants of another video (9.22 m, 450 L/s, 30 kW; 220 m, 987 kW). No research code, no name but "Pelton Lab" (once, a text note on PL-08).
- **Not shown as results anywhere**: the prototype's 200 W and the 2 kW target.

### What the sheets say they do not do

PL-04: the generator is generic (no source gives poles, slots, magnets, air gap: the lab's estimates), the model knows only a voltage constant and a resistance. PL-05 and PL-06: an example room, ratings and sizes estimated, to be checked by a qualified electrician, **not a wiring plan**; PL-06 says that the dump load, the controller, the inverter, the house, the meter's accuracy and the protections are **not simulated** (the model's battery is an ideal bus), and that a real board puts the breaker and the battery fuse on the direct side after the rectifier (the same sentence as the part sheets: the lab puts them at the head of the wall to be found first). PL-07: in the video a module carries the three threads on one slice of the core, the drawing cuts each thread in quarter-turn modules (as the 3D does). PL-08: zones are where a type is **used**, not where it is possible; the sources disagree at the edges; not a tool to size a plant.

The contract with eight sheets and four groups, the notices (each sheet says what is generic on it), thousands separators (`1,000` / `1 000`) in the parity test of the numbers, the model results recomputed from `src/sim` (generator, derived phase figures, the gap figures), PL-04 against `src/three/generator/geometry.ts` (radii, stack, pitches, tooth angles and phases, the lamination hole point by point, the coil sides, the magnets, 16 magnets and 48 coil sides drawn), PL-05 against `POWERROOM` (every device box at its place in the elevation, the walls, doorway, board, battery and rod of the plan, the order of `POWER_PARTS`), PL-06 (the estimates marked `*`, the derived `†`, the three "not simulated", the real order of protections, the ten balloons), PL-07 (the threads and the modules and bolts count against `helix.ts`, every dimension and mark), PL-08 (every zone outline, every line of equal power against `chooser.ts`, the presets and the two plants at their coordinates, the 0.75 against `src/panels/model.ts`).

### Weak or doubtful (wave B)

- **Density.** PL-04 and PL-06 are busy at 1280 px: the smallest texts are 1.35 to 1.5 mm cap height (the electrical angles under the teeth, the ratings in the diagram), readable only with the kit's zoom. PL-06 is a diagram on a big sheet: text could be larger.
- **PL-04 section A** passes through a slot, so the plane shows the yoke only and the coils are seen behind it (tinted, not hatched); a section through a tooth would show the coil in the cut. The terminal box inside (block, studs, gland) is a drawing choice, its parts are not in `dims.ts` apart from the studs and the wall.
- **PL-05**: the conduit and the cable tray have no height in `dims.ts` (they are in `room.ts`), so they are drawn without a dimension (the sheet says so); the plan shows the tray leaving the east wall through the doorway's width, it goes out **above the door** (said in the cables list).
- **PL-06** draws the breaker and the fuses on the three-phase link (as the 3D wires them) although the sheets call it a "DC breaker"; the note says what a real board does and a dashed fuse marks the usual place of the battery's.
- **PL-08**: the zones overlap so much that the labels cannot all stand in their zone (their places are chosen by hand, in the chart's own units); the legend table has the head ranges. The Kaplan, crossflow and Francis flow limits are the chooser's estimates.
